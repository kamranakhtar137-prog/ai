7. Was the Application Deleted?

Status: NOT PROVEN The audit log proves deletion of the WordPress user account, not the application record.

User accounts, application records, form entries, application statuses, and uploads can reside in separate structures. Deleting a WordPress user therefore does not establish that form or custom application data was removed.

  1. Application still exists but is no longer associated with the deleted user.
  2. Gravity Forms entry and/or entry metadata survives.
  3. The record remains as a Gravity Forms draft.
  4. The record resides in a custom application table.
  5. The record was separately deleted and must be located in backups or exports.
  6. The confirmation email was issued before final persistence completed.

8. Why the Confirmation Email Matters

The reported email is evidence that the processing workflow reached its notification stage around 17:48 on 11 August 2026. Its evidentiary weight depends on the implementation: the notification may be sent after database insertion, or before later user/application processing succeeds.

The next review should identify the source form and form ID, notification trigger, database persistence order, and any custom PHP processing that separately creates users and applications.
