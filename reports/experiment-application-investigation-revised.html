4.2 Interpretation

Thea’s case is not explained by deletion of her WordPress account: the account remains active. The evidence instead shows a saved draft that was never persisted as a final entry. The reported confirmation email requires independent verification in the transactional mail provider, because the site’s local email-log table has no matching record.

The two cases therefore point to a broader application-state/persistence issue. A user can have no visible final application even when an account remains active; Jonathan’s account deletion may be consequential, but it is not sufficient to explain this pattern.

4.3 Later administrative-context access

On 21 Aug 2026 at 11:23:44.724 UTC, Thea’s account was logged in from 89.63.10.143 with a Windows/Chrome browser. The recorded login URL was a WordPress administrator Users search page for the account email. This is consistent with administrative “login as user”/impersonation access, not an ordinary applicant login. The audit record does not capture the original administrator who initiated it.

5. Other Deleted Applicants

The audit log contains multiple deleted applicant accounts during August 2026, involving both shared/known actors and “Unknown User” records. This establishes that Jonathan’s deletion was not the only user deletion in the period. It does not establish that every deletion was erroneous or that every deleted user had an application.

A wider impact review should reconcile each deleted account with its final entries, drafts, started-entry records, and confirmation-email evidence before communicating totals or root cause.

8. Client-Safe Conclusion

Jonathan’s account was deleted on 20 August 2026 at 08:25:46 CEST by a targeted operation initiated through the Effecticore MainWP maintenance session authenticated as Effecticore_Maintenance. The available local records do not identify the individual portal user or show whether that MainWP operation was manually selected or automated. A full database backup from immediately before the deletion is available for safe staging recovery.

Thea’s account remains active, but her Canada application exists only as a recoverable draft and not as a final entry. This demonstrates that missing application data can occur independently of user-account deletion and requires a focused review of the application finalization and notification workflow.
